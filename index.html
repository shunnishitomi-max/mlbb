Meta Dashboard

The dashboard only computes professional statistics from loaded verified match records. When no verified Week 6+ records are present, it shows INSUFFICIENT DATA rather than inventing numbers.

Methodology

Coach recommendations combine available verified pick/ban evidence with draft context, role scarcity, flexibility, ally role coverage, enemy role pressure, and composition balance. Any score shown without a verified pro sample is labeled analytical inference, not official MPL or Asian Games data.

Required scope

MPL Philippines: Week 6 to latest completed match. MPL Indonesia: Week 6 to latest completed match. Asian Games 2026: completed official MLBB matches. Source identity is kept separate.

Data integrity

  • Official facts and calculated statistics are kept separate from coaching inference.
  • Unknown fields stay unknown.
  • Small or missing samples never appear as fake percentages.
  • Saved drafts remain in this browser via local storage.

Public sources

This build is prepared to consume a repository-hosted verified match JSON feed. Hero portraits and role metadata are loaded from a public hero metadata dataset. Professional statistics must be backed by match records before they affect the meta dashboard.
